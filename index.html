02 // About Me

Md. Sajan Bishwas

I am Md. Sajan Bishwas, a PhD Student and Graduate Research Assistant in Public Health Sciences at the University of North Carolina, Charlotte, in the Department of Epidemiology and Community Health. I hold an MPH from BRAC University's James P. Grant School of Public Health and MSc and BSc degrees in Public Health and Informatics from Jahangirnagar University, Bangladesh.

My doctoral research focuses on child homicide and intimate partner violence (IPV) using the National Violent Death Reporting System (NVDRS). I bring a mixed-methods lens — quantitative analysis in Stata and R alongside qualitative inquiry using NVivo and Dedoose.

Prior to my PhD, I served as Senior Project Officer at BRAC, leading WASH programming for Rohingya refugees on Bhasan Char Island. My trajectory has taken me from the slums of Dhaka to refugee camps in Cox's Bazar to the halls of UNC Charlotte.
